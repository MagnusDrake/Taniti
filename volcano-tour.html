Taniti Volcano Tour

A Breathtaking Journey

Join our expert guides for a safe and unforgettable tour of Taniti's famous active volcano. You'll hike through lush rainforest trails to reach stunning viewpoints, learn about the island's geology, and experience one of its most powerful natural wonders.

Key Information

  • Duration: 4 Hours
  • Price: $95 per person
  • Good For: Adventure, Nature Lovers
  • Location: Taniti Mountain Interior
