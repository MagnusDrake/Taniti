Snorkeling in Yellow Leaf Bay

Explore an Underwater Paradise

Discover the vibrant marine life of Taniti in the crystal-clear waters of Yellow Leaf Bay. This guided snorkeling tour is perfect for all skill levels and offers a chance to see stunning coral reefs and a wide variety of tropical fish. All equipment is provided.

Key Information

  • Duration: 2 Hours
  • Price: $45 per person
  • Good For: Families, Relaxation, Beginners
  • Location: Yellow Leaf Bay

How to Book

To reserve your snorkeling adventure, please contact Taniti Water Sports at 555-123-5678.
