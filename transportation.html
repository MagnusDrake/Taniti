Getting to & Around Taniti

All the information you need to travel to and explore the island with ease.

Arriving in Taniti

Almost all visitors arrive by air at Taniti's small but modern airport. The airport accommodates small jets and is in the process of expanding. A small cruise ship also docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.

Ground Transportation

Once you're on the island, you have several options for getting around:

  • Public & Private Buses: Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. daily, while private buses serve the rest of the island.
  • Rental Cars: Cars can be rented from a local agency near the airport for maximum flexibility.
  • Taxis: Taxis are readily available in Taniti City.
  • Bike Rentals: For exploring flatter areas like Taniti City and Merriton Landing, bikes are a great option. Helmets are required by law.
  • Walking: Taniti City and the area around Merriton Landing are very walkable and easy to explore on foot.
