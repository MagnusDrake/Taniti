Plan Your Trip: Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information to help you prepare for a smooth and enjoyable visit to Taniti.

Practical Information

What currency is used in Taniti?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar (USD) as its currency. Many businesses also accept euros and yen, and major credit cards are widely accepted.

What type of power outlets are used?

Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as in the United States. You will not need a power adapter if you are traveling from the US.

Health & Safety

Is Taniti a safe destination?

Violent crime is very rare. However, petty crimes like pickpocketing can occur, so visitors should remain aware of their surroundings, especially in crowded areas.

What are the healthcare facilities like?

There is one hospital and several clinics on the island. The hospital has many multilingual employees to assist international visitors.

Local Customs & Laws

Do people speak English?

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English, especially in tourist areas. Very little English is spoken in rural areas by older residents.

What is the drinking age?

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol is not sold or served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.

Do attractions close for holidays?

Yes. Taniti has many national holidays, and tourist attractions and restaurants are often closed. Visitors should check the holiday schedule and plan accordingly.
